Ask Krishna

Ask Krishna has two modes. Reflect lets you hold a question in mind and open the Bhagavad Gita to up to five random passages for guidance. Read Gita covers all 18 chapters.

GitHubLive DemoMIT

What it is

Ask Krishna has two modes.

Reflect follows an old practice for moments when you're torn or confused: hold your question in mind and open the Bhagavad Gita to a random page for guidance. You can open up to five random passages. If one answers you, good. If none does, take it as the Gita telling you to wait. Five is a suggestion, not an enforced limit. Your question is never typed or stored.

Before reading, a short mantra appears as a purification pause because you're about to read words spoken by the Lord Himself.

Read Gita lets you read all 18 chapters, navigate by chapter and verse, and view Sanskrit text with English or Hindi meanings.

How it is built

The site is static, with no application server. Bookmarks, your reading position, and other reading state stay on the device. Passages and verses can be saved as image cards generated in the browser. Interface strings and meanings load from static language files.

Why it exists

Unlike a chatbot, there is nothing to type and nothing to ask. You simply hold your question in mind, and a passage appears.

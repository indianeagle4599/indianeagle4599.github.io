AI and machine learning projects

Speech & ASR

AudioBench

AudioBench was built for Amplify Dental so a small team could judge speech models on real conditions — and stop redoing transcription, annotation, and benchmarking from scratch.

Computer vision

Pixtyle

Pixel Stylizer Toolkit for transforming photos and videos into stylized wallpapers using advanced computer vision techniques.

Detecting AI Generated Images

A systematic PyTorch training and tuning workflow for distinguishing AI-generated from human images, with repeatable experiment runs that preserve configs, metrics, plots, and checkpoints so each iteration can inform the next.

LLM & retrieval

Aftersight

Aftersight is an image-first search system for photo libraries. It indexes images so they can be found by meaning, visible text, or metadata — not just filenames.

LazyResume

Start with an existing resume PDF, turn it into a structured profile, and regenerate a clean, consistently formatted resume without rebuilding everything by hand.

Research

Fuzzy Hybrid Model for Stock Trading

A stock-trading research project where I used domain analysis and feature engineering to cut the predictor’s market-input channels by over 70%, then combined deep learning with fuzzy logic for trading decisions.

Tools & apps

Ask Krishna

Ask Krishna is inspired by an old practice: hold a question silently, open the Bhagavad Gita, and let the passage you meet speak to the moment. Read Gita covers all 18 chapters.

Flodoro

A local-first Pomodoro workspace that keeps the timer, tasks, history, and your own music on one static page — no account or backend required.

OS Toolkit

An expanding Python filesystem toolkit for developers, data scientists, and scripts or agents: understand storage usage, work with directory trees, and reuse common OS operations without rebuilding them from scratch. Benchmarked against copytree and robocopy.
